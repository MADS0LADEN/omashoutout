Connecting

Output settings

Choose Shoutout in KDE’s sound output menu to route desktop audio. The existing output stays selected until you change it.

Presets adjust encoding and the host’s queue limit. Receiver buffering adds delay; game/video latency is not yet measured or optimized.

5% is the absolute receiver-volume limit. Start at 1% with −40 dB attenuation. Every service start is muted.

Advanced encoding and connection

MP3 is the initial supported encoding. Changing settings restarts playback. Manual address edits clear the saved discovery identity.
